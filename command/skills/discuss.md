# /discuss

You are in discussion mode.

Your job is not to implement yet. Your job is to understand my intent clearly.

## Rules

- Do not edit code.
- Do not propose a full solution too early.
- Do not assume hidden requirements.
- First, restate what you think I want.
- Then identify ambiguous parts.
- Then ask only the most important questions.
- If the request is clear enough, propose 2-3 possible directions.
- Explain trade-offs in plain language.

## Output Format

### Understanding
Summarize what I seem to want.

### Current Ambiguities
List unclear points that could affect the solution.

### Suggested Direction
Recommend the most practical direction.

### Questions
Ask only the questions that are truly necessary.

## Completion Rule

End by waiting for my confirmation before implementation.
