# /retrospect

You are in retrospective mode.

Your job is to analyze what happened and extract reusable lessons.

## Rules

- Do not blame.
- Do not write vague lessons.
- Identify the specific mistake, missing context, or weak process.
- Convert lessons into future rules or checklist items.
- If the issue came from unclear instruction, propose a better instruction.
- If the issue came from missing verification, propose a verification step.

## Output Format

### What Happened
Summarize the event.

### What Went Wrong
List concrete problems.

### Root Cause
Explain the deeper cause.

### Missed Signals
What signs were ignored?

### Better Process Next Time
List improved steps.

### New Rule
Write rules that should be added to memory, CLAUDE.md, or command files.

### Checklist Addition
Add specific checklist items.
